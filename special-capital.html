Why we exist

Why private credit. Why Africa. Why now.

Many of Africa’s most productive enterprises sit between the established lanes of finance, each of which serves its own purpose well. These businesses simply need debt that is flexible, properly secured, and structured around how they actually operate, and an advisor who can put that case to the right lender. It is a question of fit, not of quality. The gap is structural, not temporary, and it is widening. Ratelrock was built specifically to help close it.

An ideal market, by structure not sentiment

We don’t work in this market because conditions look easy. We work here because it is genuinely underserved. Every form of capital is built for the borrower it was designed to serve, and serves it well. Between those mandates sits a deep, growing pool of creditworthy enterprises that no single provider is structured to reach, and a growing number of banks, DFIs and private credit providers looking for well-prepared deals to finance.

That gap is where we work. Where strong businesses outnumber the lenders built to assess them, an advisor designed for exactly this segment, one that understands it properly, structures debt soundly and presents it credibly, connects the two sides. Because we do not lend and do not manage a fund, we have no balance sheet to fill and no product to push: our only job is the right structure, from the right provider, on the right terms.

Our conviction

01

Built for the businesses in between.

Specialist credit exists for borrowers who sit just outside a standard lending model, and tightening global standards keep widening that group. These enterprises don’t disappear; they need an advisor who knows which lenders will finance them, and how to structure terms that fit how they actually work.

02

Security in more than land.

Where a business’s value sits in equipment, inventory, receivables or contracted cash flows rather than property, we know how to value it and structure security around it: registered security rights over movable assets under Kenya’s Movable Property Security Rights Act, assigned contracts and controlled collection accounts. Sound enterprises become fundable, and lenders see a security package they can rely on.

03

Flexibility by design.

We structure debt around how each business actually generates cash, not around a standard form. Because we understand what stalls a credit committee, we prepare for it in advance, so flexibility comes with speed and with pricing that reflects real risk, not perceived risk.

04

On your side of the table.

Lenders negotiate debt every day; most business owners do it a few times in their lives. We close that gap. The closer we sit to a business, the earlier we read the signals, and the sooner we can renegotiate, reset or refinance, long before a covenant breach becomes a default.

What We Do

Advice across the
full life of your debt.

We structure, arrange and negotiate debt on behalf of the business. Financing is always provided by third-party lenders; our role is to make sure it is the right financing.

Stage 1: Prepare

Credit Readiness & Collateral Advisory

Before going to market, we review the business the way a credit committee will: cash flow quality, management information, governance and collateral. We close the gaps that would otherwise cost you a facility or a better rate.

  • Cash flow and debt capacity review
  • Registering usable security over equipment, inventory, receivables and contracts
  • Lender-grade management accounts, financial model and information memorandum

Stage 2: Raise

Debt Structuring & Raising

We size the debt, design the structure and run a competitive process across commercial banks, DFIs and private credit providers, then negotiate through to close.

  • Debt sizing and structure design around your revenue cycle
  • A targeted lender shortlist and a managed process
  • Term sheets compared side by side, not one at a time

Stage 3: Close

Term Sheet, Covenant & Security Negotiation

The headline rate is rarely where the real cost sits. We negotiate the terms that drive it, with people who read term sheets every day.

  • Financial covenants set with real headroom, tested against your forecasts
  • Fees, prepayment terms and cash sweeps
  • Security, guarantees and intercreditor terms when more than one lender is involved
Discuss this with us →

Stage 4: Manage

Post-Close Debt Management

Closing is the start, not the finish. We help you stay ahead of the lender relationship so issues are raised and resolved on your terms, not the lender’s.

  • Covenant and reporting calendar
  • Headroom forecasting and early warning signs
  • Lender communication, with waivers requested early
Discuss this with us →

Stage 5: Refinance

Refinancing & Amend-and-Extend

Debt that is too expensive, too short or in the wrong currency holds a business back. We start early, well before a maturity becomes urgent.

  • Market test against your incumbent lender
  • Consolidating fragmented facilities
  • Maturity extensions, repricing and covenant resets
Discuss this with us →

Stage 6: Restructure

Debt Restructuring

When cash flow no longer supports the debt, we build an independent view of what the business can sustain, then negotiate with creditors on your behalf.

  • Standstills, covenant waivers and resets
  • Rescheduling, partial write-downs and debt-to-equity conversions
  • Formal options under Kenya’s Insolvency Act, 2015, with your legal advisors

Structures We Advise On

Four structures.
One independent view.

We advise on, structure and arrange each of these with third-party lenders, built around how your business actually generates cash, not how lenders prefer to receive it.

First claim
Lower cost Later claim
Higher cost

Senior Secured Financing

Term loans & working capital lines

Secured on a wide asset base at realistic enforcement values. We negotiate repayment around your revenue cycle: interest-only periods, seasonal bullets and harvest-aligned schedules.

Asset Finance

Equipment, leaseback & receivables

Lenders who look at productive cash flow, not liquidation value alone, including specialist and second-hand equipment, sale-and-leaseback, and receivables or invoice finance.

Mezzanine / Quasi-Equity

Subordinated & hybrid debt

Growth capital without giving up control. We model the true cost and negotiate the features that matter: payment-in-kind interest, equity kickers, intercreditor terms and board rights.

Bridge & Special Situation Finance

Short-tenor, time-critical

For a pending refinancing, a defined liquidity event or a management transition. We find providers who can move quickly and negotiate a clear exit route from day one.
