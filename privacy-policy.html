2. Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy is issued primarily in compliance with:

  • Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019) ("KDPA"): the applicable statute. Ratelrock, as a data controller operating in Kenya, is bound by the obligations set out in the KDPA and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021.

Where the Website is accessed by individuals outside Kenya, we apply the standards of this policy as a matter of good practice. Ratelrock is not separately registered under foreign data protection regimes (such as the EU/UK GDPR or South Africa's POPIA), and this policy should not be read as a representation of such registration.

3. Data We Collect and Why

We collect only the data necessary for the purpose for which it is collected. We do not collect data speculatively or for undisclosed purposes.

3.1 Data You Provide Directly

When you contact us by email or phone, you may provide:

  • Identity data: your name, job title, and organisation.
  • Contact data: your email address and telephone number.
  • Enquiry data: the content of your message, including a general description of your business and financing needs.

We do not operate embedded web forms that automatically transmit your data to third-party platforms. Enquiries are made by email to the addresses published on this Website, or by phone.

3.2 Technical and Usage Data (Automatically Collected)

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider and analytics tools may automatically collect device and browser data, your IP address and approximate geographic location, pages visited and time spent on them, and page performance data. This data is collected in aggregate to monitor Website performance and is not used to build individual profiles or for targeted advertising.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Responding to enquiries: to reply to your message and route it to the right person.
  • Assessing initial fit: where an enquiry relates to a potential financing or advisory engagement, to make an initial assessment of whether a further conversation is appropriate.
  • Internal records: to maintain records of correspondence for governance purposes.
  • Website improvement: to analyse aggregate usage data and improve content and performance.
  • Security: to detect and prevent fraudulent, abusive, or harmful activity.
  • Legal compliance: to comply with applicable Kenyan law, including but not limited to the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018, and any other law that may require us to retain, disclose, or report certain data.

We do not use your data to serve advertising, build commercial profiles, or share it with data brokers.

Right of accessYou may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya, accessible at www.odpc.go.ke.

10. Security

  • Encryption: all data in transit between your browser and our Website is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Access controls: access to personal data is restricted to personnel who require it for their role.
  • Infrastructure: our hosting provider maintains industry-standard security practices for its infrastructure.
  • Incident response: in the event of a breach likely to result in risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant authority and affected individuals without undue delay, as required by applicable law.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we apply commercially reasonable security measures, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted to or from the Website.
