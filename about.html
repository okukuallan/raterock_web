Who We Are

Institutional rigour.
Genuine transformation.

Ratelrock is Africa's specialist private credit and business transformation practice. We structure and arrange debt financing and provide hands-on enterprise support, combining institutional credit rigour with a genuine transformation capability.

That combination is what makes us distinctly positioned in this market. When your enterprise needs a financing partner that truly understands its complexity, Ratelrock is where you start.

Ratelrock was built specifically for this market. We structure debt financing and provide active enterprise partnership to businesses at the stage where specialist expertise and flexible financing unlock the most value.

Our Team

Led by
practitioners.

Our team has operated on every side of the credit and transformation equation. Between us, we have run a Nairobi-listed pan-African investment company through a multi-market turnaround, originated and executed deals and managed portfolios across the region, and structured complex debt, distressed-debt, and insolvency situations within demanding legal and regulatory frameworks. We pair that institutional depth with a modern analytical edge: quantitative research, financial modelling, ESG and governance discipline, and proprietary technology that turns data into faster, sharper credit decisions. The result is a single team that can assess a business, structure its capital, protect the downside, and stay close through execution.
