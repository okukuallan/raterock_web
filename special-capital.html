Why we exist

Why specialist capital. Why Africa. Why now.

We exist to match the right capital and the right structure to businesses, through private credit, financial institutions and equity providers. For many of Africa’s strongest enterprises, the problem is fit, not quality.

Our conviction

01

Built for the businesses in between.

Some strong businesses sit just outside a standard lending or investing model, and tightening standards keep widening that group. We know which providers will back them.

02

Security in more than land.

Value in equipment, inventory, receivables or contracts counts. We structure and register security over it under the applicable security laws, so providers can rely on it.

03

Flexibility by design.

We match the structure to how you earn cash, and prepare for what stalls a credit or investment committee. Where debt alone is the wrong tool, we say so.

04

On your side of the table.

Lenders and investors negotiate terms every day; owners do it a few times. We close that gap, and we act early, long before a covenant breach becomes a default.
