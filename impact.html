Four Pillars

Our commitment
to responsible capital.

Every mandate, every engagement, every client review is shaped by our commitment to sustainable, inclusive economic growth.

Social Impact

Every enterprise we work with employs real people and anchors real supply chains. Ratelrock tracks job creation and retention across our client base, measured and reported consistently. The financing we help structure flows directly into productive economic activity.

Economic Inclusion

The enterprises we serve represent an important layer of Africa's economy, mid-market businesses that anchor supply chains, distribute goods to underserved communities, and provide market access to smallholder farmers and local manufacturers.

Governance Strengthening

Every enterprise undergoes a governance assessment at the start of an engagement. Where gaps exist, our transformation practice implements board structures, shareholder agreements, financial reporting frameworks, and internal controls. Governance improvement is a defined engagement objective.

Environmental Responsibility

Africa's agribusiness sector, our primary focus, operates at the intersection of economic growth and environmental risk. We apply environmental risk screening at the start of every engagement, assess climate exposure, and support businesses in adopting sustainable practices.

Primary Sector Focus

Agribusiness is
Africa's backbone.

Across Africa, agriculture contributes up to 23% of GDP and employs over 60% of the workforce — making it the continent's single largest economic sector. In Kenya specifically, the agricultural sector contributes over 33% of GDP and employs more than 40% of the total population. It is the sector where structured credit, responsible governance, and active partnership create the most transformative impact.

The financing we help structure flows into agribusiness processors, manufacturing operations, and the value chains that connect smallholder farmers to domestic and export markets, building resilient supply chains that Africa's food security depends on.
