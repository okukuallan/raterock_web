What we do

We advise on private credit for businesses a bank cannot fit.

We help you assess your options, shape the structure and negotiate with credit funds, specialist providers and development finance institutions, so terms follow your cash flow.

We advise on pricing, security over receivables, inventory, equipment and contracts, and repayments that match how your business earns.
