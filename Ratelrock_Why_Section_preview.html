Why we exist

Why private credit. Why Kenya. Why now.

Many of Kenya’s most productive enterprises sit between the established lanes of finance, each of which serves its own purpose well. These businesses simply need something built for them: capital that is flexible, properly secured, and structured around how they actually operate. It is a question of fit, not of quality. The gap is structural, not temporary, and it is widening. Ratelrock was built specifically to close it.

An ideal market, by structure not sentiment

We don’t back this market because conditions look easy. We back it because it is genuinely underserved. Every form of capital is built for the borrower it was designed to serve, and serves it well. Between those mandates sits a deep, growing pool of creditworthy enterprises that no single provider is structured to reach.

That gap is the opportunity. Where strong businesses outnumber the capital built to assess them, a lender designed for exactly this segment, one that underwrites it properly, secures facilities soundly and manages them actively, can protect capital while earning a return that reflects real value rather than market noise. Returns here are not a bet on the macro. They are engineered through collateral, structure and discipline.

Our conviction

↗

Built for the businesses in between.

Specialist credit exists for borrowers who sit just outside a standard lending model, and tightening global standards keep widening that group. These enterprises don’t disappear; they need a partner built specifically for them, on terms that fit how they actually work.

◎

Security in more than land.

Where a business’s value sits in equipment, inventory, receivables or contracted cash flows rather than property, we are built to value and secure it, through assigned contracts and lender-controlled accounts. Sound enterprises become fundable, and every facility is properly collateralised.

◇

Flexibility by design.

We structure capital around how each business actually generates cash, not around a standard form. Because we have already solved the hindrances that stall conventional lenders, flexibility comes with speed, and with pricing that reflects the risk taken and the value we add.

◆

Capital preservation comes first.

Protecting investor capital is a core tenet of our strategy. Conservative underwriting, covenants that safeguard the downside, and portfolios stress-tested against severe but plausible downturns are designed to return principal first. Our own upside comes last, only once investors are made whole.

△

A clear institutional exit.

Seasoned loans with a proven repayment record can be aggregated and sold on to banks, pension funds and institutional investors through structured securitisation. It is a deliberate route to crystallise value and return capital, not a hope of finding a buyer later.

☀

Partnership is real-time risk control.

Our embedded support is not soft value-add; it is risk mitigation. The closer we sit to a borrower, the earlier we read the signals, and the sooner we can act to protect the position long before a problem becomes a loss.
