Why we exist

Why specialist capital. Why Africa. Why now.

We exist to match the right capital and the right structure to businesses, through private credit, financial institutions and equity providers. For many of Africa’s strongest enterprises, the problem is fit, not quality.

An ideal market, by structure not sentiment

This market is not easy. It is underserved. Strong businesses sit between the lanes of finance, and the providers who would back them struggle to find them.

That gap is where we work. We do not lend and we do not manage a fund, so we have nothing to sell. Our only job is the right capital, in the right structure, on the right terms.

The Capital Stack We Advise On

Four layers.
One independent view.

We advise on, structure and arrange each of these with third-party lenders and investors, built around how your business actually generates cash, not how providers prefer to receive it.

Debt

Senior, working capital, asset & trade finance

Secured term loans and revolving lines sized to your cash flow and collateral. Asset and trade finance lends against receivables, inventory, equipment and trade flows, so repayment follows your cash cycle.

Structured / Mezzanine

Subordinated, hybrid, quasi-equity

Ranks behind senior debt and ahead of equity, for growth without giving up control. Priced above senior debt, with part of the return paid in cash and part deferred or linked to equity. We model the true cost and negotiate the terms that matter.

Private Equity & Strategic Investors

Minority growth capital, private placements only

Minority stakes for businesses where more debt would strain cash flow. Investors typically expect board representation and protections in the shareholder agreement. We prepare the valuation and investor materials, and negotiate pricing, protections and exit routes.

DFI & Blended Capital

Concessional lines & guarantees

Development finance with softer pricing or shared risk, such as guarantees that absorb part of a lender’s first losses. It can make a deal fundable that private lenders alone would decline. We match the business to the right programme and structure the application.

  • Lender and investor grade management accounts, financial model and information memorandum
